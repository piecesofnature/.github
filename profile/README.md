**Pieces of Nature** is a calm jigsaw puzzle app for iPhone and iPad. Mountain lakes, waterfalls, canyons and coasts, cut into 6 to 150 pieces, with a new puzzle every day.

Free, no ads, no account, and nothing collected. Every photo comes from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) under an open licence, and every photographer is credited in the app.
